Open Bandra

The question

The plan reserves land for gardens, playgrounds and recreation grounds. Much of it is built over, locked away or still to be acquired. The atlas reads the plan against satellite imagery and turns the gap into an argument and a set of pilots.

The way ahead

Pilots in phases
Phase 1

Revive

Existing open spaces that are under-used or run-down, redone.

Phase 2

Activate

Reserved land that is still open and unencroached, designed and opened up.

Phase 3

Reclaim

Encroached reserved land, recovered before it is too late.

Phase 4

Create

New open space on potential sites. Coming next.
