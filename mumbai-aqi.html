Three steps for Mumbai

Incentivise · Control · Compliance
Step 01

Incentivise

Incentive FSI for prefabricated construction: PWD, SRA and MHADA to have at least a 40% prefabrication mandate. Additional floor-height allowance for off-site constructed modular units.

Step 02

Control

Contain on site: gap-free hoarding before work starts, covered stockpiles and wheel-washing at every exit. Measure in real time: live data fed to a BMC dashboard, with a trigger level that stops dusty work.

Step 03

Compliance

No dust plan, no permission: dust registration as a condition of building approval. An accountability officer: a licensed environmental officer on every site above 20,000 m² built-up.
